- Venue/instrument: OKX `BTC-USDT` spot
- Bar: one hour, confirmed candles only
- Downloaded range: 2024-01-01 00:00 UTC through 2026-09-04 12:00 UTC
- Rows: 23,461, with no duplicate timestamp or missing hourly interval
- CSV SHA-256: `7138a24041e0b232f2e9f07561abd50648c8c8f4b9e4554f8c50bd9fd0e3a693`

## Protocol

- Forty-four price, return, volatility, range, volume and calendar features
- Six-hour forward-return target
- Long/flat spot position with no leverage
- Features observed at bar close and position entered at the following bar open
- 365-day rolling training window and 60-day stitched out-of-sample windows
- Six-hour rebalancing aligned with the forecast horizon
- Cost per position side: 10 bps taker fee plus 2 bps slippage
- Observed OKX demo account spot fees: 8 bps maker and 10 bps taker
- Common out-of-sample interval: 2025-01-07 through 2026-09-04, 14,527 hours

## Diagnostics

LightGBM produced Pearson IC `-0.0121`, rank IC `-0.0067`, and 50.27% directional accuracy.
Its zero-cost return was +4.48%, falling to -1.41% at 10 bps per side and -2.55% at the default
12 bps per side. The model reduced market exposure and drawdown but did not demonstrate a stable
forecasting edge.

HistGBDT produced Pearson IC `0.0215` and rank IC `0.0360`, but its signal was too unstable. At
six-hour rebalancing, its zero-cost return was only +4.24% and became negative at 2 bps per side.
The earlier hourly-rebalanced variant generated 2,139 position changes and was rejected as a
cost-dominated result.

GRU remained negative even at zero cost and is rejected in its current configuration.

## Disposition

- Retain LightGBM as a low-exposure timing and risk-filter baseline, not as promoted alpha.
- Reject the current HistGBDT and GRU configurations.
- Defer DoubleAdapt until the feature set contains crypto-specific state and the source experiment's
  forward tradeability filter is removed from signal-time inputs.
- The next research iteration should add spot/swap basis, funding, open interest, mark/index
  divergence and order-book state before increasing model complexity.
